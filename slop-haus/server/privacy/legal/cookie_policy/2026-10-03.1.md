## Strictly necessary storage

slop-haus sets only what it needs to sign you in and keep the service secure: the session and refresh cookies, the sign-in state cookie used while you sign in with an outside provider, a record in your tab of an action waiting for you to confirm your identity, and the cookie that remembers your choices here. They are set without asking, because the service cannot work without them and this notice is how they are disclosed.
