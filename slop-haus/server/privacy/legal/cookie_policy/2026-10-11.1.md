## Strictly necessary storage

slop-haus sets only what it needs to sign you in and keep the service secure: the session and refresh cookies, the sign-in state cookie used while you sign in with an outside provider, a record in your tab of an action waiting for you to confirm your identity, the cookie that remembers your choices here, and a note in your browser of whether you opened or closed the sessions panel, so the page stays as you left it. They are set without asking, because the service cannot work without them and this notice is how they are disclosed. ## Analytics

slop-haus sets no analytics cookies today. If you agree to analytics, your browser keeps a random identifier for the length of the tab's session (session storage, listed above), so a session's error reports can be grouped; it is not linked to you and is dropped when the tab closes. Without your agreement it is not stored. Nothing else is set for analytics until you agree here. [[TODO-LEGAL: banner and preferences wording for the analytics purpose]]

## Marketing

slop-haus does not set marketing cookies or storage today, and shows no advertising. If that changes, nothing is set until you agree here, and your browser's Global Privacy Control signal is honoured as a refusal. [[TODO-LEGAL: banner and preferences wording for the marketing purpose]]
