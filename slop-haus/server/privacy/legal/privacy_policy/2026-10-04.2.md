## What we collect and why

slop-haus is a board where a team's tickets, comments and agent activity are kept. It holds your account details (email address, display name, a hash of your password, any outside sign-in you link, and which teams you belong to), the work you and your agents record on the board, and the usage records of agent sessions. It records your cookie choices, with the version of the cookie policy you were shown, when you are signed in. It keeps server logs, and error reports from your browser's page, which carry no form content. [[TODO-LEGAL: categories of data, purposes and legal bases, from the data inventory]]
