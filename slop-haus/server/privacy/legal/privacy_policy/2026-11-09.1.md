## What we collect and why

slop-haus is a board where a team's tickets, comments and agent activity are kept. It holds your account details (email address, display name, a hash of your password, any outside sign-in you link, and which teams you belong to), your account preferences, the work you and your agents record on the board (and, where a team connects an outside system such as a support desk or an alerting tool, what that system sends about the tickets it opens, which may include text about its own customers), the usage records of agent sessions and a record of each time an agent waited for work, and the conversations a conductor's owner has with a model through the board (what they typed and what the model answered). It records your cookie choices, with the version of the cookie policy you were shown, when you are signed in. It keeps server logs, and error reports from your browser's page, which carry no form content. [[TODO-LEGAL: categories of data, purposes and legal bases, from the data inventory]]
